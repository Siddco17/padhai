## A1. Divider

`Vs = 12 V` in series with `Rs = 3 kΩ` and `RL = 1 kΩ` to ground. Find `Vo` across `RL`.

\[
V_o = \frac{1}{1+3} \cdot 12 = 3\ \text{V}
\]

Current in the loop: `I = 12/4k = 3 mA`. Drop on `RL`: `3 mA × 1 kΩ = 3 V`. Same answer two ways (KVL vs divider).

## A2. Thevenin, then attach a load

Left of terminals `a–b`: `Vs = 10 V` in series with `2 kΩ`, then a `2 kΩ` to ground. Load `RL = 2 kΩ` will connect across `a–b` (top of the grounded 2 kΩ is `a`, ground is `b`).

**`Vth` (open circuit):** divider of two 2 kΩ, so `Vth = 5 V`.

**`Rth`:** kill `Vs` (short). Two 2 kΩ in parallel → `Rth = 1 kΩ`.

**With `RL = 2 kΩ`:**

\[
V_L = \frac{2}{2+1} \cdot 5 = \frac{10}{3} \approx 3.33\ \text{V}
\]

Norton dual: `In = Vth/Rth = 5 mA`, parallel `1 kΩ`. Same `VL`.

## A3. Loading (notes numbers)

Source `Vs` with `Rs = 100 kΩ`, load `RL = 1 kΩ`. No buffer.

\[
V_o = \frac{1}{101}\, V_s \approx 0.0099\, V_s
\]

About **1%** of the source gets to the load. With an ideal follower in between, `Vo = Vs` (source sees infinite Zin).

## B1. Inverting design, gain −10

Need `Rf/R1 = 10` and `R1` in 1–10 kΩ. Pick `R1 = 1 kΩ`, `Rf = 10 kΩ`.

\[
\frac{V_o}{V_{in}} = -10
\]

Friday lab 1: `Vin = 0.5 V` peak → `Vo = −5 V` peak. Midband `|G|_{dB} = 20 log 10 = 20 dB`.

## B2. Non-inverting, gain +11

Same pair: `R1 = 1 kΩ` to ground, `Rf = 10 kΩ` from out to (−), `Vin` on (+).

\[
1 + \frac{10}{1} = 11
\]

`Vin = 0.5 V` → `Vo = 5.5 V`. Still inside ±15 V.

## B3. Difference amp, `α = 5`

Need `Rf/R1 = R3/R2 = 5`. Pick `R1 = R2 = 2 kΩ`, `Rf = R3 = 10 kΩ`.

\[
V_o = 5(V_2 - V_1)
\]

Check: `V1 = 0.4 V`, `V2 = 0.6 V` → `Vo = 1.0 V`.

If the ratios are **not** matched, you do **not** get a pure difference; the general expression is the longer one in `ACD_notes_24258.pdf` p.1.

## B4. Follower vs raw divider (same as A3)

`Vs = 1 V`, `Rs = 100 kΩ`, `RL = 1 kΩ`.

| Connection | `Vo` |
|------------|------|
| Load straight on the source | `1/101 ≈ 9.9 mV` |
| Follower between source and load | `1 V` |

The follower does not “amplify”; it **stops the load from changing the source voltage**.

## Golden-rule check (inverting)

(+) grounded ⇒ `Vp = 0` ⇒ `Vn = 0` (virtual ground). `In = 0` ⇒ all of `Vin/R1` goes through `Rf`.

\[
\frac{V_{in} - 0}{R_1} = \frac{0 - V_o}{R_f} \implies \frac{V_o}{V_{in}} = -\frac{R_f}{R_1}
\]

That is the only derivation you need for lab 1.
