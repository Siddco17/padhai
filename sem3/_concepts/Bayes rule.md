# Bayes rule

`P(H|D) = P(D|H) P(H) / P(D)`. Posterior ∝ likelihood × prior. Continuous: densities instead of P.

## Why it matters here

FoML week 1–3 hole. MST-1 stop: compute a **Bayes update** on a tiny table. Foundation of [[Naive Bayes]].

## Exam form

Write prior, likelihood, evidence. Normalise. Do not skip the marginal P(D).
