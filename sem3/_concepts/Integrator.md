# Integrator

Inverting integrator: feedback C, input R. `vout = −(1/RC) ∫ vin dt` (ideal). Practical: large Rf across C to kill DC gain. Differentiator swaps R and C (noisy — know why).

## Why it matters here

Pattern 3 of 5. Linear applications unit. Lab will ask calculated RC.
