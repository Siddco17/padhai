# Wheatstone bridge

Four resistances. Balance: `R1/R2 = R3/R4` (no galvanometer current). Unbalance ΔR → small voltage; use Thevenin of the bridge for loading. Kelvin for low-R (four-wire).

## Why it matters here

MST-1: *draw* it. Lecture PDFs: *Bridge and Loading Effect*. Strain gauges live here.
