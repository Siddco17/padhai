# Vector spaces

Closed under add/scale. **Basis** = linearly independent spanning set. **Dimension** = size of a basis. Inner product → orthogonality → Gram–Schmidt → orthonormal bases. Signals live here: a sinusoid is a vector.

## Why it matters here

[[Linear Algebra]] unit 2 and the “signal-space” view of [[Signals and Systems]]. Fourier coefficients are inner products against complex exponentials — [[Fourier series and transform]].
