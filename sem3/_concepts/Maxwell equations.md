# Maxwell equations

Faraday: ∇×E = −∂B/∂t. Ampere–Maxwell: ∇×H = J + ∂D/∂t. Gauss: ∇·D = ρ. Magnetic Gauss: ∇·B = 0. Plus constitutive D=εE, B=μH, J=σE. Boundary conditions on either side of an interface.

**3 lectures.** Be fluent *before* that week. Quiz 3. Displacement current is what lets waves exist in empty space.
