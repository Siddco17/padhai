# 555 timer

Comparators + FF + discharge transistor. **Astable:** two resistors + cap, period `T ≈ 0.693(RA+2RB)C`. **Monostable:** one shot `T ≈ 1.1 RC`. Pattern 5 of 5.

## Why it matters here

ACD last unit + several lab slots. Draw the 1/3–2/3 Vcc thresholds.
