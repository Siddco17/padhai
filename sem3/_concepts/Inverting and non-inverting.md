# Inverting and non-inverting

**Inverting:** `Av = −Rf/Rin`, Zin ≈ Rin, [[Virtual ground]] at `v−`. **Non-inverting:** `Av = 1 + Rf/Rg`, Zin ≈ ∞ (ideal). Both need negative feedback.
