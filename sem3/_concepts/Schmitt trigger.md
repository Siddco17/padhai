# Schmitt trigger

Comparator with **hysteresis** (positive feedback). Two thresholds. Cleans noisy crossings. Pattern 2 of 5.

## Exam form

Find UTP/LTP from the divider on the positive input. Saturation levels ±Vsat (or rail).
