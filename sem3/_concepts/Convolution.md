# Convolution

CT: `(x * h)(t) = ∫ x(τ) h(t−τ) dτ`. DT: sum instead of integral. Flip, slide, multiply, area.

## Why it matters here

How [[LTI systems]] actually respond. Master this early — MST scoring core, IEM/audio later.

## Exam form

Draw both signals. Mark overlap as a function of t. Check edge cases (t→±∞). Do not mix CT and DT formulas.
