# Biot–Savart and Ampere

Biot–Savart: dH from a current element. Ampere: `∯ H · dl = I_encl` (plus displacement current later). Stokes links curl H to current density.

## Why it matters here

[[EMFT]] 10 lectures. Same symmetry discipline as Gauss. Vector potential A: `B = ∇×A`.
