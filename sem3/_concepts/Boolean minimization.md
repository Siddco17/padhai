# Boolean minimization

K-map for ≤4 vars; **Quine–McCluskey** up to 5 with don’t-cares (DCHD unit 1). Prime implicants → essential primes → cover.

## Why it matters here

Scoring unit and the combinational half of [[HDL]] labs. Don’t-cares are free minterms — use them.
