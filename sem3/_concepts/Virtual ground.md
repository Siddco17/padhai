# Virtual ground

Inverting config: `v+` grounded ⇒ `v− ≈ 0` (not *shorted* to ground — no current into the pin). Current through the input resistor all goes through the feedback network.

## Trap

Writing KCL at `v−` but then also claiming a real wire to ground. See [[Inverting and non-inverting]].
