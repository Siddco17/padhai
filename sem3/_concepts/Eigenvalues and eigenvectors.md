# Eigenvalues and eigenvectors

`A v = λ v`. Diagonalizable ⇒ `A = V Λ V⁻¹` (spectral decomposition if A is symmetric: `Q Λ Qᵀ`). λ = stretch along v.

## Why it matters here

[[Linear Algebra]] unit 3. Covariance eigenstructure *is* [[PCA]]. Do not compute λ without saying what the direction means.
