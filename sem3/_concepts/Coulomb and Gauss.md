# Coulomb and Gauss

Coulomb: `dE` from a charge. Gauss: `∯ D · dS = Q_encl` (D = εE in linear media). Use Gauss **only** with symmetry (sphere, infinite line, infinite sheet). Potential `E = −∇V`.
