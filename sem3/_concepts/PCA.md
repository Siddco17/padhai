# PCA

Center the data. Covariance eigen-decomposition (or [[SVD]] of the data matrix). Principal components = directions of max variance. Keep the top k.

## Why it matters here

Last [[FoML]] block *and* [[Linear Algebra]] the same week. Feature extraction / dimensionality reduction. Audio later (parked IEM) is the same math on spectra.
