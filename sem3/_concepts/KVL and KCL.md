# KVL and KCL

**KCL:** algebraic sum of currents at a node is 0 (charge conservation). **KVL:** algebraic sum of voltages around a loop is 0 (energy conservation).

## Why it matters here

Year-1 EE hole. Every [[ACD]] op-amp circuit and every [[MNI]] bridge is KVL/KCL plus [[Thevenin and Norton]]. Patch from [[sem3/_meta/remediation|remediation]] before Gaikwad ch.1–2.

## Exam form

Write polarities first. Superposition only for linear circuits. Voltage divider is KVL on a series pair — see [[Voltage divider]].
