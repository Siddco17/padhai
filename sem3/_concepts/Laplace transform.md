# Laplace transform

`X(s) = ∫ x(t) e^{−st} dt` with a **region of convergence**. Fourier is the jω axis *if* the jω axis lies in the ROC. Poles/zeros → geometric evaluation of H(jω).

## Why it matters here

SnS unit 6. MNI dump included a Laplace PDF. Unilateral Laplace for circuits with ICs. Trap: forgetting ROC (two signals can share an algebraic X(s)).
