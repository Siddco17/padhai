# Impedance

`Z_R = R`, `Z_L = jωL`, `Z_C = 1/(jωC)`. Series add, parallel as conductances. First-order τ = RC or L/R.

## Why it matters here

If NS was networks: [[ACD]] filters and [[MNI]] AC bridges. Hand-wavy `H(jω)` pairs with [[Signals and Systems]] and [[Laplace transform]].
