# Fourier series and transform

Periodic → **FS** (line spectrum). Aperiodic → **FT** (density). DT cousins: DFS / DTFT / DFT. Parseval: energy in time = energy in frequency.

## Why it matters here

[[Signals and Systems]] units 3–4. Lab already plots sinusoids (`3A.py`) — those *are* FS atoms. Always ask what the frequency content means physically.
