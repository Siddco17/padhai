# Voltage divider

Two series resistors: `Vout = Vin · R2 / (R1 + R2)` if no load on the output. With load, replace R2 by R2 ∥ RL — that is [[Thevenin and Norton]] + [[Loading effect]].

## Why it matters here

Bias networks, sensor bridges, and the “gain” of a loaded divider in [[ACD]] / [[MNI]].
