# Sampling and aliasing

Bandlimited to f_max: sample faster than **2 f_max**. Impulse-train sampling → replicas in frequency. Too slow: replicas overlap = **aliasing**. Reconstruction = sinc interpolation (ideal).

## Why it matters here

SnS unit 5. Draw the picture. DT processing of CT signals, decimation/interpolation.
