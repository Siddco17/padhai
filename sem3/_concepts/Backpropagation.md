# Backpropagation

Chain rule of [[Gradient descent]] through layers. Forward: activations. Backward: dL/dw. Perceptron / delta rule is the one-layer special case. MLP: hidden layers + nonlinearities.

## Why it matters here

FoML ~3-week ANN block (after MST-1). Re-derive once by hand. Tiny NumPy before keras.
