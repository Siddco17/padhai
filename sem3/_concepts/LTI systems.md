# LTI systems

Linear + time-invariant. Fully described by impulse response `h`. Output = [[Convolution]] of input with `h`. Eigen-signals: complex exponentials → frequency response `H(jω)` or `H(e^{jω})`.

## Why it matters here

Heart of [[Signals and Systems]]. Causality: `h(t)=0` for t<0. Stability (BIBO): `h` absolutely integrable / summable.
