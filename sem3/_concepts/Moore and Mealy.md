# Moore and Mealy

**Moore:** output = f(state) only. **Mealy:** output = f(state, input) — can react in the same cycle. Overlapping vs non-overlapping sequence detectors differ by that extra input.

## Why it matters here

[[DCHD]] sequential unit and viva gold. Common trap: drawing a Mealy machine and writing Moore output logic.
