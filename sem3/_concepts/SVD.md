# SVD

`A = U Σ Vᵀ`. Singular values = sqrt of eigenvalues of `AᵀA`. Best rank-k approximation (Eckart–Young).

## Why it matters here

[[PCA]] is SVD on the (centered) data matrix. Dimensionality reduction, beamforming, and the LA “channel modeling” outcome.
