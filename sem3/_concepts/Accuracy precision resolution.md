# Accuracy, precision, resolution

**Accuracy** — closeness to true value (bias). **Precision** — scatter / repeatability. **Resolution** — smallest distinguishable change. **Sensitivity** — slope of the calibration curve. **Linearity** — deviation from the ideal straight line.

## Why it matters here

MST-1 stop condition for [[MNI]]. Mixing these four is the classic trap.
