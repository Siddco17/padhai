# Gradient, divergence, curl

**Grad** ∇f — steepest increase (potential → E). **Div** ∇·A — source / flux density (Gauss). **Curl** ∇×A — circulation (Faraday, Ampere). **Laplacian** ∇². Identities: ∇×∇f = 0, ∇·(∇×A) = 0.

## Why it matters here

[[EMFT]] lectures 1–8 and every later unit. Physical meaning, not just formulas. Operators change with [[Coordinate systems]].
