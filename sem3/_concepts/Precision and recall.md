# Precision and recall

**Precision** = TP / (TP+FP) — of predicted positives, how many true. **Recall** = TP / (TP+FN) — of actual positives, how many caught. F1 = harmonic mean.

## Why it matters here

FoML metrics, cold. Lab writeups. Imbalanced classes make accuracy a lie.
