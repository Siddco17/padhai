# Coordinate systems

Pick the system that matches **symmetry**: plane/box → Cartesian; long wire/cylinder → cylindrical (ρ, φ, z); point/sphere → spherical (r, θ, φ).

## Why it matters here

First 8 [[EMFT]] lectures. Wrong coordinates = wrong dl, dS, dV and a zero on Gauss/Ampere.

## Exam form

Write dl, dS, dV for the system. Check which components of E or H must be zero by symmetry *before* integrating. Operators in that system: [[Gradient divergence curl]].
