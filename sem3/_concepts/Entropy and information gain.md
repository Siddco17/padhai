# Entropy and information gain

`H = −Σ p log2 p`. Gain = H(parent) − weighted H(children). Higher gain → better split. Gini = `1 − Σ p²` (CART default; Kurhekar used entropy).

## Why it matters here

You cannot fake a [[Decision trees|tree]] numerical without this.
