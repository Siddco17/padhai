# Dot and cross product

**Dot** `A · B = |A||B| cos θ` — work, flux through a surface, “how much is along”. **Cross** `A × B` — perpendicular, right-hand rule, torque, force on a charge `F = q(v × B)`.

## Why it matters here

[[EMFT]] every week. Flux = ∯ D · dS. Ampere / Faraday line integrals. Lorentz force.
