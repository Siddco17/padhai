# Impulse and step

Unit impulse `δ` sifts: `∫ x(t) δ(t−t0) dt = x(t0)`. Step `u` is the running integral of `δ`. Any signal is a stack of impulses → that is why `h` determines an [[LTI systems|LTI]] system.

## Why it matters here

SnS unit 1 and lab (impulse properties). Trap: calling δ “infinity” with no sifting.
