# Gradient descent

Iterate `θ ← θ − η ∇L(θ)`. η too big: diverge. Too small: crawl. Newton uses the Hessian when the syllabus hits it (Boyd).

## Why it matters here

LA optimization unit. [[Backpropagation]] is just GD on a neural net via chain rule. Do not open Boyd until this week.
