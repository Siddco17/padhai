# Projection

The projection of `b` onto a subspace is the unique closest point in that subspace. For a matrix A with independent columns, `P = A(AᵀA)⁻¹Aᵀ`. Error `b − Pb` ⟂ Col(A).

## Why it matters here

This *is* [[Least squares]]. Linear detectors and “signal in noise” language in the LA outcomes.
