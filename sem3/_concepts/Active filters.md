# Active filters

Op-amp + RC to get poles without inductors. First/second order LPF, HPF, BPF, notch, all-pass. Design up to 6th order (equal-component / unity-gain methods in the syllabus).

## Why it matters here

Pattern 4 of 5. Paper first, then Multisim. Trap: order vs cutoff algebra. [[Impedance]] of C is `1/jωC`.
