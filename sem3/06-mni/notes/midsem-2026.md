## 1

**(a)** Voltmeter sensitivity \(1.5\,\mathrm{k}\Omega/\mathrm{V}\) reads 80 V on its 100 V range, in series with a milliammeter, across an unknown resistor. Ammeter reads 15 mA.

1. Apparent resistance
2. Actual resistance, neglecting milliammeter resistance
3. Error due to voltmeter loading
4. Percentage relative accuracy

**(b)** Periodic signal on a first-order instrument, time constant 3 s. Dynamic error of \(\pm 5\%\) is allowed. Highest frequency.

## 2

**(a)** Second-order accelerometer, to measure a sinusoid below 100 Hz. Dynamic error \(\pm 5\%\) allowed. Choose the sensor for damping ratio 0.6.

**(b)** Construction and working principle of a PMMC galvanometer.

## 3

**(a)** Output response of a first-order system to a ramp.
