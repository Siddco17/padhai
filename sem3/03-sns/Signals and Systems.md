Do not drop class. 30 min notes. Convolution + LTI early — that is the scoring core.

## Units

1. CT/DT signals — energy/power, [[Impulse and step]], sinusoids
2. [[LTI systems]] — [[Convolution]] integral/sum, impulse response
3. [[Fourier series and transform]] — FS/FT, Parseval, properties
4. Time–frequency — frequency response, group delay
5. [[Sampling and aliasing]]
6. [[Laplace transform]] and [[Z-transform]] (ROC)
