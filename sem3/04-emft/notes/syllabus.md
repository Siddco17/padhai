## Teaching plan (44 lectures)

| # | Topic | Lectures | CO |
|---|--------|----------|----|
| 1 | **Vector calculus** — Cartesian, cylindrical, spherical; differential lengths, surfaces, volumes; physical meaning of curl, divergence, gradient, Laplacian | 8 | 01 |
| 2 | **Electrostatics** — Coulomb; E, D; Gauss + applications; divergence theorem; potential + gradient; dipole + moment; energy in electric field | 10 | 02 |
| 3 | **Steady magnetic fields** — Biot–Savart; Ampere + applications; curl + Stokes; B and flux; scalar and vector magnetic potentials | 10 | 02 |
| 4 | **Maxwell + time-varying fields** — Faraday; displacement current; Maxwell in point and integral form; retarded potentials; boundary conditions | 3 | 03 |
| 5 | **Uniform plane waves** — Maxwell in phasor form; wave equation (general + perfect dielectric); solutions; η, v, λ; conductors vs dielectrics; skin depth; Poynting | 8 | 04 |
| 6 | **Reflection of EM waves** — normal incidence; standing waves; laws of reflection; oblique incidence; Brewster’s angle | 5 | 05 |

Unit 4 is only **3 lectures**. Be fluent in Faraday / Maxwell / BCs before that week; there is no time to learn it from scratch in class.

## Recommended books (from the sheet)

1. Hayt Jr. — *Engineering Electromagnetics* (TMH)
2. Sadiku — *Elements of Electromagnetics* (Oxford) — **primary; PDF already in `resources/`**
3. B. Raju — *Electromagnetic Field Theory* (Pearson)
4. K.D. Prasad — *Antennas and Electromagnetic Fields* (Khanna)
5. Griffiths — *Introduction to Electrodynamics* (PHI)
6. Schaum’s Outline — *Electromagnetic Theory* (TMH)

**Handwritten extras on the sheet:** Irodov for numericals; Feynman Lectures for intuition.

**Use:** Sadiku daily. Hayt for the Physics-hole start (coords / Coulomb / Gauss). Schaum or Irodov when you need extra drill. Griffiths / Feynman only if a concept will not stick. Skip Raju and Prasad unless lectures track them.
